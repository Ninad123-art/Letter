What to do before you reach the vet

Never give human medicine. Paracetamol can kill a cat, and ibuprofen is dangerous for cats and dogs.

FurPaws gives general guidance for healthy pets. It does not replace a veterinarian. If your pet seems very unwell, go to a vet.
